Scarlet Series Mobile Applications
Scarlet series applications are interactive educational game applications to cover the subject areas of Mathematics, Knowledge and Understanding of the World, Urdu and English concepts to establish a foundation of knowledge for our children. These applications feature multiple interactive and fun learning games, each designed to teach a specific concept. The content specifically chosen for ECE children helps to develop their literacy and numeracy skills and encourages their curiosity. All of the games include voiced instructions, so our young children will find it easy to learn and play for making something in the real rather than the virtual world.
